This pre-release refines clouds, cinematic autofocus, sunrise/sunset shadows and air/underwater light shafts. It includes all beta.4 water and High reflection improvements.

- Evolving cloud billows, finer Medium/High ray sampling and preserved clear skies at noon/midnight in dry weather.
- Rounded cinematic blur with depth-aware sampling and focus-dependent magnification; focused subjects and foreground remain protected.
- Loader-aligned solar/shadow directions, smooth horizon shadow fading, softer grazing-angle shadows and smaller camera-grid steps.
- Filtered light shafts across their whole path, actual ray-distance integration, DH opaque-depth clipping, and underwater attenuation/refraction response.

Validation includes canonical package checks, water/cloud/beta.5 preprocessing, GLSL compilation in CI, native macOS rendering checks and Mesa software OpenGL regression checks. These checks validate shader calculations; they do not measure Minecraft FPS or confirm final in-game visual/temporal quality. Increased cloud/ray sample budgets add GPU work.

Install `Lumina_Event_Horizon_v1.4.0-beta.5.zip` in the shaderpacks folder and reload the shader. Cinematic Autofocus is optional under Cinematics → World Blur Settings. Existing saved quality settings remain active. The ZIP contains `shaders/` at its root and the license. The SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside it.
